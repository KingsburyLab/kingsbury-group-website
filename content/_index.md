<p class="lead">We seek to <strong>accelerate development</strong> of electrochemical technologies that address environmental challenges related to climate change, water scarcity, and food security.</p>

Materials that filter charged particles (ions) are essential components of many clean water and energy technologies, including processes for **water desalination, wastewater recycling, energy storage,** and **energy production**.

## Research Approach

Our signature research approach integrates experiments, simulations, and software to advance fundamental understanding of ion transport phenomena in engineered, ion-selective materials such as membranes and sorbents.
